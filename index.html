4 sessions. One blueprint.

01

“Sino Ka?”

Figure out what you want to be known for.

  • Pick 3 words you want people to know you for
  • Find 3 real stories that prove it
  • Turn each story into a resume bullet
  • Practice a 30-second intro
02

“Proof on Paper”

Show what you can do, on paper.

  • Fix your resume bullets
  • Edit your LinkedIn, live
  • Pick your proof projects
  • Write one message to a real person
03

“Proof in Public”

Plan how people will see your work.

  • Find your 4 content pillars
  • Write 20 post ideas
  • Match a real story to each pillar
  • Plan four weeks of posts (posting stays optional)

Facilitator: Kurt Boquiren

04

“Ako ’To”

Finish one post. Then show it to people.

  • Finish one post, ready to publish
  • Turn it into two more drafts
  • Show and explain your work, face to face
  • Leave with a pod and an accountability partner

Walk out with something to show for it.

Every session ends with something you keep. Here’s where each one comes in handy.

  1. A career-brand statementOne clear line on what you’re about. It anchors your resume, LinkedIn, and intro.
  2. A 3-story proof bankReal stories, ready for interviews, essays, and “tell me about yourself.”
  3. A 30-second introFor career fairs, interviews, and the first minute of any meeting. No more freezing up.
  4. Stronger resume bulletsBullets that show what you did and what changed because of it.
  5. A better LinkedIn introA headline and About section a recruiter will read to the end.
  6. One outreach messageWritten for a real person you want to learn from, and ready to send.
  7. 4 content pillars and 20 ideasEnough to know what to post about for the next few months.
  8. A four-week content planOne post a week, mapped out. Post when you’re ready, or keep it for later.
  9. One finished post and two draftsReady to publish whenever you decide to.
  10. An accountability partnerSomeone from your cohort who checks in with you weekly for a month after the workshop.

Questions, answered.

Who is Blueprint for?

College students in any year: org applications, internships, scholarships or a first job. Fresh grads are welcome too.

Is it just about resumes?

No. It covers your career-brand statement, résumé, LinkedIn, a content plan, and meeting people. Posting online is optional.

Is it a lecture?

No. You work on your own material the whole time: on your own, in pairs, and in quick speed rounds.

What do I need to bring?

A laptop or phone for every session. For Session 2, bring your current résumé and your LinkedIn link. We’ll email short pre-work before some sessions.
