Who is Blueprint for?

College students. Mainly 4th years. Fresh grads and 3rd years getting ready for internships are welcome too.

Is it just about resumes?

No. It also covers personal branding, LinkedIn, content creation, networking, and getting ready for jobs.

Is it a lecture?

No. You work on your own stuff the whole time — on your own, in pairs, and in quick speed rounds.

For Session 2, bring your resume and your LinkedIn link.
