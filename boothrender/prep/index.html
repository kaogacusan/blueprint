The banner. Sized to fit the stand.

A roll-up (pull-up) stand holds the print at the top and the bottom: the bottom is stuck to a spring roller inside the base, and the top is gripped by a rail that hooks on a pole. So the stand and the print are two separate things, and the print has to match the stand.

  1. Get the stand. Message the FB seller (under Buy): “Is the 80 × 200 roll-up still available? Does it come with the top rail, pole, and bag? Pickup or Lalamove today?”
  2. Measure it. When it comes, measure the top rail end to end. 80 cm: our file is right. 85 cm: message Keira and the file gets re-made at 85 × 200 in a few minutes. Don’t print before you know.
  3. Print. Tell the shop: “Roll-up banner print, 80 × 200 cm, no grommets, no hem, cut exact.” Tarp is fine; PP film curls less. File: pullup/v1.pdf ↓
  4. Fit it. Easiest: bring the stand to the shop and ask them to install the print (most do). Yourselves: keep the locking pin in the base, peel the tape strip on the roller, stick the print’s bottom edge on it straight, then pull the pin out slowly so it rolls in.
  5. Stand it up. Turn the feet out, click the pole together and stand it in the base, pull the print up slowly, and clip the top rail onto the pole. Outside the front-left tent leg, angled up the road.

Prince’s banner is 60 × 160 cm on Atasha’s stand: the same rule applies. Measure the stand, then print.
