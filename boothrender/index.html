Who stands where.

Seller

  • At the table the whole time the booth is open.
  • Runs the 1-minute AAA demo. Signs people up on the QR or the sheet.
  • Wears the cash pouch, if we take cash.

Roamer

  • In the aisle and around campus with the banner, at class breaks.
  • Gives out flyers. Sends people to the booth.
  • Rolls the banner and keeps it by the back table when not out.

What we know. What we still check.

Rules we know

  • 1.5 m × 3 m per group. Two groups share one 3 × 3 m tent. Official
  • Bazaar week is Nov 9 to 13. Canvas, LAS 50, week of Nov 3
  • Booths are manned at all times. A panel grades the booth. Canvas, LAS 50, task 5.4
  • Only the tent is given. No tables or chairs. Gelo’s plan, 20 Oct, not confirmed
  • Selling runs 9 am to 4 pm. Gelo’s plan
  • Booths line both sides of the road. An e-jeep lane runs along it since 2025. Keep the banner and the roamer out of it. RecWeek 2026 map · The GUIDON, 2024

Still to check

  • Our stall number. Stalls are not given yet.
  • Which group shares our tent. Who brings the divider.
  • The leg setting. Ask for 2.0 m or more at the eave.
  • Do we take payment at the booth? If no, drop the GCash QR and the cash pouch.
  • Can the tarps stay in the tent overnight?
  • Final tarp text. The print on the model is a draft.
