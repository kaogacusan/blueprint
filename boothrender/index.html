Mon, Nov 9 · set-up

  • Be there 45 minutes before selling starts, with 3 people.
  • Bring the tarps, the banner and stand, the table, a stool, a sturdy chair to stand on, and the supply box.
  • Follow the 7 steps below, in order.

Every day

  • Open: banner out and tied to the leg, signs and cards on the table, phone charged, one test scan.
  • Close: banner in. Signs, cards, clipboard, and tablecloth go into the supply box, and someone takes it home. The tarps stay only if the organiser says so.

How to set it up · about 45 minutes, 3 people

  1. Check the tent. Our half is the left half, facing the road. Ask for the legs on the top setting: the front bar should be 2.0 m up or more.
  2. Header tarp (2 people, a sturdy chair, 15 min).
    • Lay it face down. Put a zip tie through every grommet on the bottom edge. Don’t close them yet.
    • Hold it against the outside of the front bar, print facing the road, the bottom edge along the bar. Close the zip ties around the bar, middle first, then out to the ends. Pull tight and snip the tails.
    • Tie a 1 m rope to each top corner. Pull each one back and tie it to the nearest roof pole, so the tarp stands up straight.
    • No roof poles to tie to? Hang it from the back bar instead, behind the seller. Never under the front bar: it would hang down to 1.4 m.
  3. Table (5 min). Front left of our half, 25 cm in from the front edge. Supply box under it. Tablecloth on, centred, the same drop on both sides.
  4. Table skirt (5 min). Over the cloth, on the road side. Zip-tie the 2 top grommets to the front table legs just under the top, then the 2 bottom ones lower down. Keep it flat.
  5. Table top (5 min). QR sign back left, workshop card back right, bingo cards and pens front left, clipboard front right. Same as the “Table setup” view.
  6. Standing banner (5 min). Just outside the front-left tent leg, angled up the road. Roll-up: open the feet, click the pole together, set it in the base, pull the banner up and hook it on top. X-stand: open the legs and hook the 4 corners. Zip-tie the pole to the tent leg.
  7. Test (5 min). Scan the QR on 2 phones. Open the sign-up page on the seller’s phone. Send a photo of the booth to the group chat.

Supply box

  • Zip ties (100) and scissors. 2 m of rope. Duct tape.
  • Spare bingo cards, 5 sign-up sheets, the clipboard, 3 pens and a cup.
  • Power bank and cable.
  • The two signs in their stands, and the tablecloth, at closing.

Who does what

  • Seller, at the table the whole time: hands out bingo cards and pens, starts the chat, pitches the Barkada deal (3 for ₱1,999), signs people up on the QR or the clipboard.
  • Floater, in the aisle at class breaks: hands out bingo cards and sends people to the booth. Opens the banner in the morning and brings it in at closing or when it gets windy.
