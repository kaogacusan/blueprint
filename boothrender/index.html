Set it up. About an hour, 3 people.

Selling may start Monday, Nov 9: be there an hour before, with the tarps, the banner and stand, and the supply box. Thomas brings the table.

In this order

  1. Check the tent. Our half is the left half, facing the road. Ask for the legs on the top setting: the front bar should be 2.0 m up or more.
  2. Header tarp (2 people, a sturdy chair, 15 min).
    • Lay it face down. Put a zip tie through every grommet on the bottom edge. Don’t close them yet.
    • Hold it against the outside of the front bar, print facing the road, the bottom edge along the bar. Close the zip ties around the bar, middle first, then out to the ends. Pull tight and snip the tails.
    • Tie a 1 m rope to each top corner. Pull each one back and tie it to the nearest roof pole, so the tarp stands up straight.
    • No roof poles to tie to? Zip-tie a 1 m stick (bamboo or PVC) upright to the front bar at each end of the tarp, and tie the top corners to the sticks. Never hang it under the front bar: it would come down to 1.4 m, head height.
  3. Backdrop tarp (2 people, a sturdy chair, 10 min).
    • From inside the tent, hold it against the back bar, print facing the road, centred on our half.
    • Zip-tie every grommet on the top edge around the back bar, middle first, then out to the ends. Snip the tails.
    • So it doesn’t flap: tie the bottom-left corner to the back-left tent leg. The right corner has no leg near it, so hang a full water bottle from it on a short string. The bottom edge sits about 0.5 m off the floor.
  4. Table (5 min). Thomas’s Lifetime table, front left of our half, 25 cm in from the front edge. That leaves about 28 cm on the right to get behind it. Supply box under it. Tablecloth on, centred, the same drop on both sides.
  5. Table skirt (5 min). Over the cloth, on the road side, centred (the table is 1.22 m, the skirt 0.9 m). Zip-tie the 2 top grommets to the table’s front edge frame, then tape or tie the bottom corners to the legs. Keep it flat.
  6. Table top (5 min). Back row, centre: the sign-up QR in its A4 stand. Front row, left to right: bingo cards and the pen cup, flyers, calling cards, the clipboard. A laptop, if someone brings one, goes back right. Same as the “Table setup” view.
  7. Roll-up banner (5 min). Just outside the front-left tent leg, angled up the road. Turn the 2 feet out, click the pole sections together and stand the pole in the base, then pull the banner up slowly and hook its top rail on the pole. Zip-tie the pole to the tent leg in wind. To pack: unhook, and let it roll down slowly.
  8. Test (5 min). Scan the QR on 2 phones. Open the sign-up page on the seller’s phone. Send a photo of the booth to the group chat.

Supply box

  • Zip ties (100) and scissors. 2 m of rope. Duct tape.
  • Spare flyers, bingo cards and calling cards, 5 sign-up sheets, the clipboard, 3 pens and a cup.
  • Power bank and cable.
  • The QR sign in its stand, and the tablecloth, at closing.

Who does what

  • Seller, at the table the whole time: hands out bingo cards and calling cards, pitches the Barkada deal (3 for ₱1,999), signs people up on the QR or the clipboard. Laptop on the site if there is one.
  • Floater, in the aisle at class breaks: hands out flyers and invites people to try the bingo card at the table. Opens the banner in the morning and brings it in at closing or when it gets windy.

What we know. What we still check.

Rules we know

  • 1.5 m × 3 m per group. Two groups share one 3 × 3 m tent. Official
  • Bazaar week is Nov 9 to 13. Canvas, LAS 50, week of Nov 3
  • Booths are manned at all times. A panel grades the booth. Canvas, LAS 50, task 5.4
  • Only the tent is given. No tables or chairs. Thomas brings a Lifetime table. Gelo’s plan, 20 Oct, not confirmed
  • Selling runs 9 am to 4 pm. Gelo’s plan. Prince’s plan says 8 am to 5 pm
  • Booths line both sides of the road. An e-jeep lane runs along it since 2025. Keep the banner and the floater out of it. RecWeek 2026 map · The GUIDON, 2024

Still to check

  • When we can set up (Sun or Mon), and what time selling starts.
  • Our stall number, and which group shares our tent.
  • The leg setting. Ask for 2.0 m or more at the eave.
  • Can the tarps (header and backdrop) stay in the tent overnight?
  • Can the banner stand just outside the tent, at the front corner? Keep it out of the e-jeep lane either way.
  • Do we owe a tent fee? Prince’s group paid ₱1,126.76.
  • Are chairs given? If not, members bring 1–2.
  • Is Thomas’s table the 4 ft one? A 6 ft one won’t fit our half.
